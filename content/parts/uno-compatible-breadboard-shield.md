## About

Breadboard shields reduce the need for numerous long jumper wires by situating a breadboard on top of the Uno for quicker prototyping. The breadboard sits on a grid of exposed conductive material, including two pairs of ground and 5V lines. The digital and analog input/output pins bracket the breadboard, allowing for swift, small connections.
